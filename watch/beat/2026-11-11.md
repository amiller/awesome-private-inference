Timeline: first misuse of stolen data Oct 22; second server tampered Nov 4–5; containment Nov 5; **KMS root key rotated Nov 6**; a researcher's canary credentials (planted since Jul 1) traced the exposure Nov 8; containment completed Nov 8; misuse observed as late as Nov 9. Phala asks OpenRouter users of that model in the window to rotate all secrets in their request contents, and says a full-platform audit found "no evidence of compromise affecting other models or products"; a full RCA is promised.
