  [verifier-v0.6.5](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/dstack/releases/tag/verifier-v0.6.5)
  (04:25Z), plus component kms/gateway/auth-eth v0.6.5 images; and the 0.5
  line's [v0.5.15](https://github.com/Dstack-TEE/dstack/releases/tag/v0.5.15)
  (03:39Z). This ships yesterday's KMS-enforced launch tickets (PR #1577,
  reported 2026-11-15):
  - `DstackApp.setRequireLaunchTicket`/`addLaunchTicket` gate app keys and
    certificates; `DstackKms.setRequireKmsLaunchTicket` gates a joining KMS's
    root-key import; the VMM keeps the ticket and returns only its hash; the
    ticket is never placed in `vm_config`, which attestations embed.
  - **Breaking:** `AuthorizationRequest` gains `launchTicketHash`, changing
    the `authorizeApp`/`authorizeKms` selectors and the `IAppEpochAuth`
    interface ID (`0xd33835bd` → `0xc396c1b0`). Contracts, auth-eth, KMS and
    apps must be upgraded together; a 0.6.4/0.6.5 mix denies every request.
    Fixed upgrade order in the release notes: contracts + auth-eth, then KMS,
    then gateway, then guests (register the new `os_image_hash` first).
  - v0.5.15 adds only the guest side of launch tickets; without a ticket
    requirement nothing changes, and 0.5.14-or-older guests are refused once
    an app requires one.
