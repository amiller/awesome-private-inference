  pinned 2026.06.09 (1.2.2) to release 2026.11.23 (2.1.0, `da4949b`), needed

  an OCSP-freshness patch upstream still lacks); `libnvat` SONAME becomes
  `libnvat.so.2`. Validation: standalone builds on Debian trixie and Ubuntu
  24.04 plus the mkosi acceptance suite passed; full image build and GPU
