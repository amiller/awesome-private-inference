## Research and attacks

- **Mior — SEV-SNP reverse-map attack with forged attestation (ETH Zurich,
  disclosed 2026-11-10, CCS '26).** A hypervisor/BIOS-level attacker
  redirects PSP writes to the Reverse Map Table during SEV-SNP startup to a
  PCIe device, leaving part of the RMP uninitialized; the attacker then
  rewrites guest protections, uses PSP debug commands to read/write VM
  memory, and can forge signed attestation reports (a benign VM's measurement
  was copied into a malicious VM). Demonstrated end-to-end on Zen 5 EPYC 9135
  (SEV-SNP firmware API 1.58.4), 100% in 1,000 attempts; Zen 3/4 potentially
  affected per source analysis. Tracked as CVE-2026-0462, CVSS v4 5.9,
  covered by AMD-SB-3059 (firmware fix available; OEM BIOS targets Dec
  2026–Feb 2027). Reported to AMD 2026-02-12 under a 2026-11-10 embargo —
  so it dates from the AMD/CCS disclosure wave, but it is new to this beat's
  record, which had nothing after DDRop/BreakFAST. Paper:
  [xca-attacks.github.io/mior](https://xca-attacks.github.io/mior/)
  ([PDF](https://xca-attacks.github.io/mior/mior_ccs26.pdf)), PoC:
  [github.com/xca-attacks/mior](https://github.com/xca-attacks/mior);
  secondhand summary: [The Hacker News, this week](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/new-mior-and-skyfall-attacks-let-rogue.html).
- **Skyfall — startup-failure residual-state leak on SEV-SNP (ETH Zurich +
  Google, disclosed 2026-11-10).** A malicious hypervisor deliberately fails
  a VM's startup, then reads residual cached VM state (registers, memory,
  disk-encryption keys) via Transient Scheduler Attacks at three fixed
  cache-line offsets. Zen 3 and Zen 4 EPYC demonstrated; no attestation
  forgery. CVEs: CVE-2026-40671 (SEV leak, 4.9, AMD-SB-3061) and
  CVE-2026-18448 (SMM leak, 5.6); mitigations in 2026-08-12 microcode plus
  pending OEM BIOS. Paper:
  [skyfall-attack.github.io](https://skyfall-attack.github.io/assets/skyfall.pdf).
- Related AMD-SB-3064 brief (RowHammer against SEV-SNP metadata with ECC
  disabled — keep ECC enabled) is part of the same disclosure wave, above.
- Nothing else new: arXiv cs.CR listings (2026-11-13 batch) contain no
  processor-TEE attack or attestation-verification papers. DDRop (KU Leuven,
  CCS) and the rest of the standing record unchanged. CCS 2026 (Nov 15–19,
  The Hague) opens in two days; Mior/Skyfall are part of its program.
