Observatory View Previews

Five candidate views, each drawn from our real snapshots and archives. A and B are the fleet overview in the OONI style, C and D are Wayback-style calendars for a single route or machine, and E is a monitor card in the style of Cloudflare's Key Transparency page. Hover any mark for its details.

A

One row per provider and model, one column per day since Apr 21. Before Oct 14 our verifier only reported pass or fail, and several of those passes were later shown to be unsupported, so they are drawn pale. From Oct 14 the rewritten verifier reports verified, rejected or not checked.

B

The same data stacked per provider, as OONI draws it

after OONI MAT, rows = providers

Each bar counts that provider's routes by outcome for one day. This view hides which route failed but shows how much of each provider we can check on a given day.

An alternative to A when the number of routes grows past what fits on a screen.
