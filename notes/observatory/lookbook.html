Observatory Lookbook

These are 26 public observatories, monitors and registries whose designs fit a Private Inference Observatory. They are grouped by kind: rating sites, long-running measurements, permanent records, and flows and events. All screenshots were taken on Nov 24. Merkle Town, Cloudflare's certificate transparency monitor, now lives inside Cloudflare Radar, and its two transparency pages were captured through a regular browser session. Electricity Maps and Software Heritage blocked the capture, so they are left out.

Six design moves to borrow first

A stage badge whose hover lists the missing criteriaFrom L2BEAT.→ stage criteria per route
An overall score with every check scored under it, at a named commitFrom OpenSSF Scorecard.→ client registry
Rows of entities by columns of daysFrom OONI, with the query kept in the URL.→ 212 daily snapshots
The recent window and the full record side by sideFrom NOAA's Mauna Loa record.→ identity and TCB history
A calendar of the days that were capturedFrom the Wayback Machine.→ evidence retention
A permanent record page with aliases and the raw JSONFrom OSV.→ finding pages

Rating sites grade each target against stated criteria, and the best show the checks behind the grade

L2BEAT

Rates Ethereum rollups against published stage criteria. l2beat.com now redirects to ethbeat.org.

Borrow

The stage badge. Hovering it names the stage, lists the items missing for the next one, and says that stages are not a measure of security. Also the daily anomaly strip.

fits: stage criteria per route; daily pass/fail per provider

Avoid

The summary explains almost nothing without hovering, and project pages run to about twenty sections.

OpenSSF Scorecard

Scores open-source repositories on supply-chain security checks.

Borrow

An overall score with every check listed under it, each with its own score, a risk weight (critical, high, medium) and a short explanation, plus the commit and date the report was generated at.

fits: client registry, reviewed at a pinned commit; per-route checks

Avoid

An aggregate score invites readers to stop at one number.

Can I use

Shows which browser versions support each web feature.

Borrow

A feature-by-client grid coloured by support level, with numbered footnotes inside the cells and a tab for known issues. Partial support is a first-class state.

fits: verifier grid; check-by-client parity table with teep

Avoid

Dense version ranges become hard to read on small screens.

Repology

Tracks which version of each package every distribution ships.

Borrow

One row per repository with the version it carries, coloured newest, outdated or legacy. It shows at a glance who is behind.

fits: which reference values each client's allowlist carries versus the newest observed

Avoid

The rows run long and have no summary at the top.

Mozilla HTTP Observatory

Grades a website's security headers.

Borrow

A grade shown with the number of checks behind it and a trend since the last scan, with raw headers, history and a benchmark one tab away.

fits: each client-by-provider cell of the verifier grid

Avoid

Scores that can exceed 100 out of 100.

clientdiversity.org

Tracks the share of Ethereum validators running each client.

Borrow

Bar tracks shaded with the goal and danger thresholds, a toggle between independent data sources, and a "data is stale" banner.

fits: comparing what teep, tinfoil-go and our adapters conclude about one route

Avoid

No history: only the current share is shown.

Artificial Analysis

Benchmarks LLM API endpoints across hosts.

Borrow

The endpoint, meaning one provider serving one model, as the unit of a row, so the same model appears under each host.

fits: routes in the Private Inference Beat table

Avoid

Density, paywalled features, and no raw evidence.

Measurement sites show the same quantity daily for years, and the strongest put the raw data one click away

OONI Explorer, Measurement Aggregation Toolkit

Aggregates network censorship measurements from volunteer probes.

Borrow

One row per entity, shared day columns, and an independent scale per row. Every filter lives in the URL, each chart has JSON and CSV links, and each measurement opens as its own page.

fits: the 212 daily snapshots by provider and model

Avoid

A bad filter combination returns a bare "No Data Found".

NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory

Publishes the Mauna Loa carbon dioxide record, kept since 1958.

Borrow

The same series at two time scales side by side, the last few years and the full record, under a callout comparing this month with the same month last year. Data, growth-rate and PNG or PDF versions are each one tab away.

fits: gateway identity and TCB history: last 30 days beside the full record

Avoid

The page is long, and the explanatory text sits below the charts.

Google Transparency Report, HTTPS

Measures how much Chrome traffic is encrypted, by platform, since 2015.

Borrow

A plain explanation of why the measure matters, placed above one long-running percentage line per platform.

fits: share of routes whose evidence verified, by provider, over time

Avoid

A lot of scrolling before the first chart.

Let's Encrypt stats

Publishes daily counts of active certificates and certificates issued.

Borrow

A last-updated date at the top, a zoom and download toolbar on each chart, and a chart that openly reuses another observatory's data with credit.

fits: daily counts of observations and new identities

Avoid

Charts sit one after another with little to say what each shows.

Reproducible Builds CI

Reports whether each Debian package rebuilds bit for bit.

Borrow

An entity page where status and raw artifacts sit to one side and the diff that explains a change fills the main pane.

fits: per-machine deployment logs; diffs on a new identity

Avoid

A dated look and a cryptic weather-icon legend.

Our World in Data

Publishes charts of long-run global statistics with their sources.

Borrow

A source line under every chart that gives the last update and the next expected update, Table, Map and Line tabs, and an entity picker beside the chart.

fits: every chart; "next expected update" maps onto the probe cadence

Avoid

A cookie banner covers the foot of the chart on first load.

mevboost.pics

Tracks relay and builder market share for MEV-Boost.

Borrow

100% stacked daily share, with the share that can't be attributed drawn as its own muted band.

fits: days a route was unreachable or not checked

Avoid

The latest slot was 18 days old with no staleness warning, and two series share a color.

OpenRouter rankings

Ranks models by tokens served through OpenRouter each week.

Borrow

Weekly stacked volume by model over a year, with a ranked list beneath giving each model's share and its change since last week.

fits: weekly newly observed identities, stacked by provider

Avoid

Dozens of stacked colours can't be told apart.

Epoch AI data hub

Tracks notable AI models and their training compute.

Borrow

An "Updated" date badge, a prominent download button, a graph and table toggle, and axes the reader can choose.

fits: every chart on the observatory site

Avoid

A cookie banner sits over the chart on first load.

ultrasound.money

Tracks ETH supply and burn.

Borrow

One timeframe toggle that controls every chart on the page.

Avoid

Gauges and slogans carry most of the page, and it shows no evidence.

Record sites give every entry a permanent page with its identifiers, aliases and raw data

Cloudflare Radar: Key Transparency and Certificate Transparency

Cloudflare's monitors for the logs behind WhatsApp and Messenger keys, and for TLS certificates. ct.cloudflare.com, once Merkle Town, now redirects here.

Borrow

The monitor card. Each log shows the last epoch it signed next to the last epoch an independent party verified, with the time of that check, the current root hash, and whether the log is online. A disabled log keeps its card with the date it was last verified.

fits: one card per provider reference publication or per machine, with "published" beside "last verified by us"

Avoid

The certificate page loads its lower charts lazily, and on our visit they stayed as grey placeholders.

Sigstore Rekor Search

Searches the Sigstore transparency log.

Borrow

The entry card. It shows the UUID, type, log index and integrated time (both relative and absolute), then the hash, signature and decoded certificate in monospace.

fits: an on-chain compose-hash addition; a firmware endorsement

Avoid

An empty search that says only "No matching entries".

OSV

An open database of vulnerabilities across package ecosystems.

Borrow

A canonical record page with the ID as its heading, its aliases in other databases, published and modified dates, the raw JSON inline with a link to fetch it, and every reference.

fits: a finding's own page, cross-linked to the provider issue and the teep issue

Avoid

A long list of references with no indication of which matter.

deps.dev

Google's package insight service for open-source dependencies.

Borrow

A provenance box beside the package that says Verified and lists the repository, build config, workflow and transparency-log entry, with instructions for checking it yourself.

fits: an image's provenance on the machine page, such as a Sigstore entry for a release

Avoid

The provenance box is easy to miss at the side of a dense page.

Internet Archive Wayback Machine

Archives web pages over time.

Borrow

A captures-per-year histogram above a full calendar where each captured day is marked, under a single line: saved 472 times between April 9, 2021 and November 15, 2026.

fits: retention coverage: the days we hold raw evidence for each machine or route

Avoid

The calendar shows that a capture exists, not what changed.

AI Incident Database

Catalogues reported harms and failures of AI systems.

Borrow

A searchable incident catalogue with filters for classification, source, incident date and published date, and table, list, entity and taxonomy views of the same records.

fits: findings and incidents the daily digest catches, such as the SGLang RCE

Avoid

The card view leans on stock news images.

relayscan.io

Reports relay and builder statistics.

Borrow

Plain tables with the time window in the URL, a snapshot timestamp, copy-as-markdown, and a caveat placed beside the figure it qualifies.

fits: daily digest tables

Avoid

No charts at all.

Flow and event sites draw a pipeline or a live feed, and tie the list to the picture

orderflow.art

Flashbots' map of Ethereum order flow from frontend to block builder.

Borrow

A filter directly above each stage of the pipeline, a switch between two volume measures, and a methodology page with a numbered list of known limitations.

fits: the trust chain from hardware vendor to firmware, image, gateway and model route

Avoid

Thin grey links that are hard to follow, a long tail labelled by hex address, and no export.

USGS Earthquake Hazards

Maps earthquakes worldwide as they are reported.

Borrow

A live event list kept in step with the picture, with the magnitude as the first thing on each row and the time in UTC.

fits: the feed of newly observed identities and TCB changes beside the timeline

Avoid

The map takes most of the space, and ours has no geography to show.
